# OffCulture — Early Beta Release

**Version:** 0.1.0-beta  
**Status:** Early Access  
**Website:** [offculture.live](https://offculture.live)

---

## What's in this release

This is the first public preview of OffCulture — a platform connecting people with vibe-curated spaces for community meetups and co-working.

### Included in Beta
- Browse and discover curated cafes and spaces near you
- Filter by vibe, capacity, and availability
- Submit a space request for your community or group
- Real-time booking status updates

---

## Known Limitations

- This is an early-stage prototype — some features may be incomplete or change without notice
- Venue availability is currently limited to select locations
- Booking confirmations are handled manually by the OffCulture team
